# 🔄 My Analytical Approach

I approach data problems using an end-to-end analytical workflow:

**1. Define the problem**
Understand the business, operational or research question.

**2. Acquire & validate data**
Assess data structure, completeness and quality.

**3. Clean & transform**
Prepare analysis-ready datasets using SQL, Python or Power Query.

**4. Explore & analyse**
Identify trends, relationships, anomalies and important performance indicators.

**5. Model where appropriate**
Apply statistical or machine-learning approaches where they provide meaningful additional insight.

**6. Visualise & communicate**
Build clear reports and interactive dashboards for technical and non-technical audiences.

**7. Translate analysis into action**
Focus on what the evidence means for decision-makers, not simply what the numbers show.
